Timing

Press Start, then hit any fret, pad or key (or tap the screen) as each note reaches the line, along with the click. Play for 30 seconds. The notes slide to match you as it learns your delay.

Fine-tune by hand

Using a TV? Switch it to game mode first. It can cut 50–100 ms of lag. Screen delay moves the notes without changing when hits count: use it if the notes and the click look out of step. The headset has its own screen delay.
